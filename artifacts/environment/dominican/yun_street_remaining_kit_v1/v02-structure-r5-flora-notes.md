# Yun street remaining kit — v02 structure r5 flora

Status: **AWAITING OWNER REVIEW**

This correction replaces the insufficient tree silhouettes shown in r3/r4.

- Royal palms now use smooth tapered trunks, visible growth rings, a crownshaft, three frond height layers, curved rachises, and paired lance-shaped leaflets.
- Tall and short palms have different trunk and crown proportions.
- The shade tree now follows the horizontal tiering of a tropical almond tree, including visible branch levels and a split flattened upper crown.
- The compact street tree remains intentionally smaller and more tightly pruned.
- Bougainvillea and potted shrub retain the improved r4 planter, stem, and clustered-growth construction.
- The r3 and r4 checkpoint files remain preserved for comparison.

Not started: final plant materials, color variation, leaf optimization/LOD, GLB export, or Yun runtime mounting.
