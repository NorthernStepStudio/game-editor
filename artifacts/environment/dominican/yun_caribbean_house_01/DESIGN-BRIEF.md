# Yun Caribbean House 01

Original, standalone, game-ready Caribbean urban concrete house for Yun.

## Art direction

- Colorful but grounded Caribbean street architecture.
- Stylized, readable proportions with clean shapes and modest lived-in character.
- Warm faded tropical paint, sand/off-white concrete accents, dark metalwork.
- Subtle repaired plaster only; no heavy decay, fantasy, futurism, or copied real building.

## Building contract

- Narrow two-story reinforced-concrete house retaining the original authored proportions. The legacy design envelope is baked at the locked Yun building scale `0.55`, producing a native gameplay asset approximately `4.0339 × 6.4103 × 4.037` units.
- Flat roof, rooftop parapet, small water tank, second-floor balcony, metal railing.
- Exterior staircase, barred windows, metal doors, gated entrance, ground-floor awning.
- Wall-mounted air conditioner, electrical conduit, meter box, drain and utility details.
- Complete front, sides, back, and roof geometry.

## Runtime contract

- Standalone building only: no road, sidewalk, terrain, vegetation, vehicles, people, or neighboring structures.
- World origin at the center of the ground plane; no geometry below Z=0; exported root and child scales are `1.0`.
- Simple collision covers the closed shell, gate, exterior stair/landing and reachable guard boundaries.
- Mobile-friendly mesh/material budget, no textures, no unnecessary micro-detail.
- Source `.blend`, exported `.glb`, six review renders, manifest, and validation report.
- Production-ready but unmounted. Live-map placement and release promotion remain separate approvals.
