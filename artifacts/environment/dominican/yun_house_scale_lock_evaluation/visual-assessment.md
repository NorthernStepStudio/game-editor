# Visual scale assessment

## Scale comparison

- **0.55 — Pass:** doors provide clear headroom for Paco, Tito and Neno; balcony guards read around chest height; stair risers fit Tito's body scale; windows are usable; floor height is compact but believable; the garage remains unmistakable.
- **0.60 — Too large:** still workable, but balcony guards, window heights and floor spacing begin to feel oversized beside the shipping roster.
- **0.65 — Too large:** the extra height is clear in both the gameplay and close boards; stories, doors and railings drift back toward the original mismatch.

## Selected 0.55 feature checks

- House 01 door: 1.177 game units, approximately 1.46 Tito-heights.
- House 02 door: 1.183 game units, approximately 1.47 Tito-heights.
- House 02 garage opening: 1.265 game units; wide and tall enough to retain its storefront/garage read.
- House 01 stair rise: 0.143 game units, approximately 18% of Tito's height.
- Balcony guards: approximately 0.506–0.512 game units, around 63% of Tito's height.
- Approximate floor-to-floor height: 1.689 game units, about 2.10 Tito-heights.
- Neno, the tallest measured current character, still has visible door clearance.

## Decision

Use one shared Yun neighborhood-building scale of `0.55`. Do not assign different values per house. Do not alter the current candidate assets until native-size conversion is separately approved.
