# Yun Caribbean House 02

Second original neighborhood house for Yun. It must share House 01's stylized Caribbean concrete-home language while being identifiable by silhouette and facade arrangement rather than color alone.

## Distinguishing design

- Two-story, shallower concrete home with no exterior stair.
- Asymmetric ground floor: broad garage/storefront shutter on the left, pedestrian entry and slab shade on the right.
- Recessed upper floor and left corner balcony with a plaster curb, solid end piers, and denser metal pickets.
- Paired narrow upper windows, different side/rear openings, stepped rooftop parapet, squat rooftop tank.
- Rear AC unit and visible front utility meter/conduit.
- Maintained, mildly faded wall plaster with restrained repair patches.

## Production targets

- LOD0: approximately 5,000–8,000 triangles when visual quality permits.
- LOD1: a simpler distance version preserving the main silhouette.
- Four reusable materials: wall/plaster, dark metal, glass/window, detail/roof.
- Complete front, sides, rear, and roof; ground aligned to Z=0.
- No street, sidewalk, people, cars, vegetation, or unrelated props in the exported asset.
- Candidate only; no Yun map replacement or promotion.
