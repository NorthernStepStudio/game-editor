## Differences seen against the sheet (proposed corrections for v02-structure)

1. **Hair** is too small and tight. The sheet has big, messy, curly volume around the face and a high ponytail
   with an orange scrunchie, not a braid-like tail.
2. **Jacket sleeves** should be long, puffy and gathered at the wrist with black cuffs (they stop at the elbow here).
3. **Jacket** should be oversized and boxy, with black panels at the elbows and a triangle emblem on the back.
4. **Sneakers** need to be chunkier high-tops with thick, shaped soles (they are flat blocks here).
5. **Legs** should taper (thigh → knee → calf) instead of straight tubes; the thigh strap on the left leg is missing.
6. **Face**: larger anime eyes and more defined cheeks and chin.
