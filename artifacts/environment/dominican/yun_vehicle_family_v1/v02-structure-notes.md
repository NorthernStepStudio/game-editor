# Yun vehicle family — v02 structure

Status: **AWAITING OWNER REVIEW**

Seven vehicles remain together in the visible Blender review scene: sedan, taxi, sport sedan, sport hatchback, SUV, van and delivery vehicle.

This checkpoint adds the major vehicle forms only:

- tapered cabins and differentiated rooflines;
- front, rear and side glass openings;
- bumpers, fenders and primary lower-body forms;
- taxi roof sign;
- sport-sedan splitter and side skirts;
- supported sport-hatch roof wing;
- SUV roof rails and cargo mass;
- van sliding-door structure;
- delivery cab, cargo box, rear roll-door mass and cab steps.

All seven assets are centered locally around reusable roots. Their roots place them on the review grid. Current Yun vehicle meshes were used only to measure the existing gameplay footprint; no third-party geometry is part of these candidates.

Not included until `v03-details`: lights, grille patterns, mirrors, handles, door seams, wheel hubs, plates, wipers, vents, exhausts and other secondary details.
